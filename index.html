Reading the model

Servicing at 10% of a US$175k device is about US$17.5k a year per installed unit. Consumables at US$25 a scan only match that above roughly two scans a day, every day. The two recurring streams end up similar in size and both small, so for the rest of the decade this is mostly a capital equipment business. First Responder grows into the largest single line in the base case, but it is also the furthest from clearance and the least evidenced.

The deck's US$7.5 to 30M device revenue figures are line capacity times price, with the company's note that they are not a forecast. Selling 150 units a year into roughly 800 Stroke Belt targets means reaching about a fifth of them every year. No early medtech capital launch does that in its first two years, which is why the ramp, not the price, drives every number here.

Not included: reimbursement outcomes (NTAP excludes capital cost), sales force and operating costs, the time hospitals take to approve capital purchases, international sales, or the 2027 readout failing. Nothing here is a forecast or advice.
